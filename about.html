About Espila Mail

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Espila Mail is a precious, premium and unique email service, calm and private, for people who value their own space.

Why is it precious?

It is not open to everyone. Every account is given personally, by invitation only, in the member's own name.

What makes it premium and unique?

Your mailbox opens in your own language: 8 Indian languages, English and 65+ international languages. The experience is quiet and uncluttered.

Is there a charge?

Espila Mail is complimentary for those invited. There is no charge in the present Seed phase.

How do I join?

Request an invitation on our home page with your full name and preferred address.
